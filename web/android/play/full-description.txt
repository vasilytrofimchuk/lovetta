Lovetta — Your AI Girlfriend

Someone who listens. Who remembers. Who's genuinely happy to hear from you.

With Lovetta you bring someone to life. Pick one of 18 unique characters — realistic or anime — or design your own: her look, her voice, her personality and her name.

She remembers what you told her last week and picks up right where you left off. She sends you selfies and voice messages made just for you, and you can talk to her with your voice too.

WHAT YOU GET
• Natural conversations with an AI character who has her own personality and memory
• AI-generated selfies and voice messages
• Voice input — just speak to her
• Several companions, each completely different

SUBSCRIPTION REQUIRED
Lovetta is a subscription app. A Premium subscription is required to create a companion and to chat — there is no free tier and no free trial.
• Monthly — $19.99 / month
• Yearly — $99.99 / year (save 58%)
Prices may vary by country. Payment is charged to your Google Play account. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

All characters are fictional and AI-generated, and all of them are adults. Conversations are generated by AI and may be inaccurate; Lovetta is entertainment, not advice.

ACCOUNT
Delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete Account; this removes your conversations and personal data. Support is available inside the app.

For adults 18+ only.
